# Provenance and caveats — DV2 supermarket showcase

1. ScrapeHero, *Number of Woolworths Supermarkets locations in Australia in 2026*, snapshot 5 October 2026, state-by-state count: https://www.scrapehero.com/location-reports/Woolworths%20Supermarkets-Australia/ . The dataset represents one reporting company's estimates, not an authoritative census.
2. ScrapeHero, *Number of Coles Supermarkets locations in Australia in 2026*, snapshot 28 September 2026, state-by-state count: https://www.scrapehero.com/location-reports/Coles%2520Supermarkets-Australia/ . Excludes other Coles Group stores.
3. Australian Bureau of Statistics, *National, state and territory population, March 2026*: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/national-state-and-territory-population/mar-2026 . For per-capita store comparisons, population dates do not exactly match store-count snapshots. All state populations here use ABS March 2026 rounded values, not ScrapeHero population denominators.
4. ACCC, *Supermarkets inquiry 2024–25 interim report*, Figure 1.1, estimate of national take-home food and grocery sales in FY2002–03, FY2006–07 and FY2022–23: https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/supermarkets-inquiry-2024-2025-interim-report.pdf . CAUTION: figures are estimates of share of **national take-home food and grocery sales** not market shares of store numbers. Metcash category covers wholesale supply to independent supermarkets. Do not interpret it as one retail chain. Other retailers is an aggregate. Numeric totals may differ from 100 slightly due rounding.
5. Australian Bureau of Statistics, *Retail Trade, Australia, June 2025*, Food retailing monthly seasonal adjusted turnover series: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/retail-and-wholesale-trade/retail-trade-australia/jun-2025 . This includes ALL FOOD RETAILING (supermarkets, liquor and specialised food stores), not grocery supermarket sales alone. Units are AUD $ million, current prices, seasonally adjusted. Nominal sales changes are not the same as quantities purchased or inflation-adjusted spending. ABS ceased this publication following June 2025.
6. Genuine state boundary geometry: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tonywr71/GeoJson-Data/master/australian-states.json . Used under source terms; verify if attribution requirements change.

**Scope**: Two large supermarket chains only for the geography and location count charts. Locations outside these chains (ALDI, IGA etc) are omitted; do not interpret counts as all Australian supermarkets. The Dorling cartogram (chart 03) and fourth tile map (chart 04) are *schematic*, while the choropleth and proportional-symbol maps use actual boundary geometry. The fourth tile map is an optional supplemental map; its independent classification is not guaranteed. Original tile map in approximately geographic order; not a true distance or area map. The second map anchors each state's total at an approximate representative location, not at store coordinates.
