CHAPTER 01 — THE FOOTPRINT

Where are Australia's big supermarkets?

Raw store totals favour populous states. Compare store availability relative to the latest ABS population estimates, then explore the footprint through three different spatial approaches.

01
ADVANCED · CHOROPLETH MAP

Stores relative to population

Coles + Woolworths supermarkets per 100,000 residents by state.

Read the colour: darker states have more outlets per resident—not necessarily better real-world access.
02
ADVANCED · PROPORTIONAL SYMBOL MAP

Where most stores sit

Each circle represents a whole state's combined chain total.

Circle area = outlets. Symbols are anchored approximately within each state, not at precise shop addresses.
03
ADVANCED · DORLING CARTOGRAM

If every state was a circle, how big would it be?

Each state or territory becomes a circle. Circle area represents its number of Coles and Woolworths supermarkets; colour represents the Woolworths proportion of those two chains.

Distinct from the symbol map: state polygons are replaced entirely by non-overlapping circles arranged in an approximate geographic layout. This is a deliberately schematic, state-level cartogram; it does not show actual stores or distances.

ADVANCED · WAFFLE CHART

About 57 of every 100 grocery-sales dollars

The ACCC estimated that Coles and Woolworths together accounted for 57.3% of national take-home food and grocery sales in 2022–23.

How to read: each square stands for roughly one percentage point. The 57 coloured squares round the exact 57.3% figure; this is a proportion of grocery sales, not a share of stores or shopping trips.

CHAPTER 03 — THE SPENDING

The food economy keeps moving.

Australia's reported food retailing turnover moved higher from 2020 to 2025. These are nominal sales figures—part of that increase may reflect higher prices, not more groceries in the trolley.

11
BASIC · AREA / LINE CHART

Five years of food retailing

ABS food retailing also includes specialist food stores and liquor retailers. This is a national nominal turnover measure, not household grocery spending.

THE TAKEAWAY

Store count isn't the same as shopper choice.

Coles and Woolworths are present across all eight states and territories. But geographic totals hide regional gaps, and the ACCC's separate market estimates remind us that size and purchasing power are more than just the number of locations. For a stronger final assignment, the next step would be to add postcode-level accessibility or comparable grocery-basket price data.
